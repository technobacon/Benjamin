## Four-pass audit

### Accuracy

- [ ] Important claims appear in the ledger.
- [ ] Numbers have units, periods, and denominators.
- [ ] Current facts were checked at the stated cutoff.
- [ ] Interpretation is not presented as settled fact.

### Argument

- [ ] Each section has a distinct job.
- [ ] The causal links are explicit.
- [ ] The strongest counterargument receives a fair hearing.
- [ ] The conclusion follows from the evidence.

### Voice

- [ ] The draft sounds natural aloud.
- [ ] Generic AI phrasing and repeated conclusions are gone.
- [ ] Humour is brief and well placed.
- [ ] A non-gamer can follow every gaming reference.

### Production

- [ ] Visual cues are specific and feasible.
- [ ] Charts identify units, dates, and sources.
- [ ] Franklin supports rather than crowds the evidence.
- [ ] The title and thumbnail promise are fulfilled.
